# Project COOL
### Cooling Optimization for Online Intelligence Labs

## About the Project

Project COOL is a Data Science project focused on improving cooling decisions in data centers. Data centers generate heat while processing and storing data, and their cooling systems may consume significant amounts of electricity and water.

Instead of focusing only on reducing AI usage, this project explores how data centers can use smarter cooling strategies based on environmental conditions, server workload, and water availability.

The goal is to develop a data-driven decision-support system that recommends suitable cooling strategies while considering energy efficiency, water consumption, and safe operating temperatures.

## Problem Statement

Data centers support many digital services, including artificial intelligence, social media, video streaming, and cloud computing. These facilities require effective cooling to maintain safe operating conditions.

However, cooling requirements and resource consumption vary depending on factors such as outdoor temperature, humidity, server workload, cooling technology, and local water availability.

Project COOL aims to explore how these factors can be used to make better cooling decisions.

## Objectives

- Study existing data-center cooling technologies and their limitations.
- Analyze the factors that affect cooling requirements.
- Explore the relationship between weather, server workload, water availability, and cooling performance.
- Develop a decision-support model to recommend suitable cooling strategies.
- Compare potential cooling strategies based on energy use, water consumption, and thermal requirements.
- Evaluate the proposed approach using available datasets and test scenarios.

## Proposed Approach

The project will follow these steps:

1. **Research:** Study existing cooling technologies and related research.
2. **Data Collection:** Identify suitable datasets for weather, server workload, water stress, and cooling performance.
3. **Data Analysis:** Clean and analyze the collected data to identify relevant patterns.
4. **Model Development:** Develop a rule-based decision model to recommend suitable cooling strategies.
5. **Prototype Development:** Build an interactive dashboard to display inputs, recommendations, and comparisons.
6. **Evaluation:** Test the model under different conditions and compare its recommendations against a baseline.

## Key Factors

The proposed system will consider:

- **Weather Conditions:** Outdoor temperature, humidity, and other relevant environmental factors.
- **Server Workload:** IT power consumption, workload, and available temperature measurements.
- **Water Availability:** Local water stress and water-use constraints.
- **Cooling Performance:** Energy requirements, water consumption, and thermal safety.

The availability of these variables will depend on the selected datasets.

## Technologies

The planned technologies include:

- **Python:** Data processing, analysis, and decision-model development.
- **Pandas:** Data cleaning and manipulation.
- **Data Visualization:** Charts and graphs for analyzing results.
- **Excel:** Supporting analysis and summary dashboards.
- **R:** Additional statistical analysis and visualizations, where useful.
- **Streamlit:** Potential framework for developing the interactive dashboard.

The final technology stack may change depending on project requirements and data availability.

## Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a working prototype that recommends suitable cooling strategies under different environmental and operational conditions.

The project will aim to:

- Present cooling recommendations with clear explanations.
- Compare potential energy and water trade-offs.
- Identify conditions under which different cooling strategies may be suitable.
- Support more informed and resource-aware cooling decisions.

The effectiveness of the proposed approach will be evaluated through data analysis and scenario-based testing. Actual resource savings will not be assumed without supporting evidence.

## Project Scope

The initial scope focuses on data analysis, cooling-strategy recommendations, and prototype development.

Live data-center integration, real-time sensor monitoring, automated cooling control, and advanced machine-learning models may be considered for future development.

## Current Status

**Status:** Research and planning phase.

The current work focuses on reviewing existing research, identifying suitable datasets, defining the methodology, and planning the first working prototype.

## Future Scope

- Integrate real-time weather and server-monitoring data.
- Explore machine learning for predicting cooling demand.
- Improve recommendations using measured cooling-system performance.
- Investigate hybrid cooling strategies.
- Evaluate the model using real data-center measurements, where available.

## Conclusion

Project COOL explores how data analysis and intelligent decision-making can help data centers manage cooling requirements more efficiently. By considering environmental conditions, server workload, water availability, and energy use, the project aims to support informed cooling decisions without compromising thermal safety.
